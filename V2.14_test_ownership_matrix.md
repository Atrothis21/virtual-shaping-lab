- `guard`: boundary/policy enforcement for architecture seams
- `contract`: public/runtime/schema contract assertions
- `behavioral`: canonical phenomenon signatures
- `unit/internal`: component-level behavior checks
- `obsolete legacy`: candidate for retirement in V2.14 retirement slices

## Candidate Deletion Policy

3. CI guard + contract + behavioral gates remain green after removal.
