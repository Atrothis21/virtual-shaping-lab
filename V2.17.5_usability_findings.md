4. Builder onboarding is less intimidating with guided stepper

- Impact: better transition from launcher to builder.

- Observation: some cards still contain multiple secondaries (`Run preset + report`, `More presets`).
- Impact: mild cognitive load persists on detailed preset surfaces.
- Action: evaluate secondary-action consolidation in Phase 6+ once recovery UX is finalized.
