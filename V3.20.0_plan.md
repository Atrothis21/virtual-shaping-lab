# V3.20.0 Plan - Policy Contract Ownership and Legality Foundation

## Objective
Establish canonical policy contract ownership under `vsl/agent/policy/*`, keep runtime policy contracts transport-only, add legality-first policy instantiation boundaries, and formalize typed agent-protocol boundary contracts (`TaskInput`, `Action`, `Outcome`, `TrialRecord`).

---

## Entry Criteria
- V3.19.15 single-path observation enforcement is merged and green.
- Learner and observation canonical ownership/instantiation patterns are stable and available for policy mirroring.
- No existing runtime policy seam bypasses canonical `vsl` ownership boundaries.
- Agreement on narrow, typed, causal interaction boundary:
  - experiment emits task-facing state
  - agent returns action
  - experiment returns outcome (not error)

### Entry Criteria Status (Start of V3.20.0)
- V3.19.15 closeout artifacts are present:
  - `docs/v3_19_15_single_path_observation_architecture.md`
  - `docs/v3_19_15_pr_evidence_checklist.md`
- Observation and learner canonical ownership surfaces are established in code:
  - `virtual_shaping_lab/vsl/agent/observation/spec.py`
  - `virtual_shaping_lab/vsl/agent/learning/spec.py`
- Existing policy package currently contains only primitive action-space/null-policy surfaces:
  - `virtual_shaping_lab/vsl/agent/policy/action_space.py`
  - `virtual_shaping_lab/vsl/agent/policy/null_policy.py`
- No canonical `PolicySpec` grammar surface existed prior to this slice.
